**What challenged you?**

**Key learning:**
A modern web page is a collection of resources. Vulnerabilities or secrets aren't always in the `index.html`. You must look at the **Stylesheets (.css)** and **Scripts (.js)** to get the full picture of what the application is doing—or what the developer accidentally left behind.
