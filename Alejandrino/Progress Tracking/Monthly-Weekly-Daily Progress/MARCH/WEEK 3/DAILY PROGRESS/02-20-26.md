## 5. Reflection (Qualitative)

**What went well today?**
I tackled the "Head Dump" challenge, which focused on manipulating HTTP request headers. I successfully used Burp Suite to modify the `User-Agent` and `Referer` headers to meet the server's specific requirements to grant access to the flag.

**What challenged you?**
The challenge required specific header values that weren't immediately obvious. I had to pay close attention to the server's error messages, which hinted at what "browser" or "site" it expected the request to come from.

**Key learning:**
Web servers don't just look at the URL; they look at the metadata in the HTTP headers. By spoofing headers like `User-Agent`, `Referer`, or `X-Forwarded-For`, an attacker can bypass basic filters that are meant to restrict access based on the source of the request.

**Tomorrow's focus:**
Continue exploring more complex HTTP header manipulations, such as **Cookie injection** or **Host Header Injection**.
