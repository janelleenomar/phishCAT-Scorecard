## 5. Reflection (Qualitative)

**What went well today?**
I solved the "Inspect HTML" challenge by using the browser's Inspect element tool to dig through the raw HTML structure. This reinforced the habit of looking past the rendered UI to see exactly what the server is sending to the client.

**What challenged you?**
While the challenge was straightforward, it served as a good reminder that information can be hidden in plain sight. Sometimes the most complex-looking web page has the most basic vulnerability sitting in a `<div>` or an attribute.

**Key learning:**
The Document Object Model (DOM) is your map. Developers often use comments or hidden attributes to track versioning or leave notes for other team members, which can accidentally leak flag fragments or internal naming conventions. Always expand the `<head>` and check the bottom of the `<body>`.

**Tomorrow's focus:**
Moving beyond simple HTML inspection into **Client-Side Cookie Manipulation** or exploring more complex **Information Disclosure** files like `.env` or `.DS_Store`.
