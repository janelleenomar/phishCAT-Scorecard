**Key learning:**
"Semantic Leaking" is a massive vulnerability in AI Data Loss Prevention (DLP). If a developer instructs an AI to mask sensitive data with contextually appropriate fake words, they inadvertently give attackers a trail of breadcrumbs. By analyzing the thematic category of the fake words, an attacker can use deductive reasoning to guess the protected secret.
