## Weekly Challenges & Lessons

**Biggest challenge this week:**
Navigating deep "rabbit holes" and encountering geographic dead ends. From getting distracted by fake social media profiles during the Capstone, to assuming airport locations without checking municipalities, and finally, staring at a Google Maps location trying to find a hotel that had been demolished years ago.

**How you overcame it (or plan to):**
I developed intense investigative discipline. Instead of guessing, I relied on structured methodology: inspecting page source code for hidden text, using exact-match Google Dorks (`intext:`) to filter out noise, and pivoting to historical news searches to account for urban development.

**Most valuable lesson learned:**
The cascading nature of OSINT and the concept of "Temporal OSINT." A single OPSEC failure—like a hardcoded copyright tag or a reused avatar—can deanonymize a target completely. Additionally, realizing that maps only show a snapshot of the world today; if a photo or video is old, you must account for changes in the physical landscape over time.

**Adjustment for next week:**
After heavily focusing on Image Intelligence (IMINT) and geolocation, I plan to shift my focus toward tracking digital footprints across platforms. I will dive deeper into Social Media Intelligence (SOCMINT) and potentially explore dark web reconnaissance.
