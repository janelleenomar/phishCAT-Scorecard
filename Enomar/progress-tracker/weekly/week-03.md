**Biggest challenge this week:**
Time management was the ultimate bottleneck. Because it was Intramurals week, I was heavily occupied with school duties and simply did not have the hours required to dive into complex new challenges or set up new environments.

**How you overcame it (or plan to):**
Instead of forcing myself to rush through new material with limited focus, I pivoted my strategy. I dedicated my single 2-hour window entirely to consolidation. I organized my existing writeups to make them easily referenceable and re-ran past challenges under a time limit to build muscle memory.

Drilling those alternative, more efficient command chains under time pressure proved that repetition is the true key to mastery.
